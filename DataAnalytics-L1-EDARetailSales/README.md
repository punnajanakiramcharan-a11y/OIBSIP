# EDA on Retail Sales Data — Superstore Dataset

## Objective
Perform exploratory data analysis on a retail sales dataset to uncover patterns, customer behavior trends, and actionable business insights.

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset, sourced from Kaggle (by Rohit Sahoo).

## Analysis Performed
1. Initial inspection: shape, dtypes, null check
2. Descriptive statistics for numerical columns
3. Time series analysis: monthly and quarterly sales trends (2015-2019)
4. Customer segment analysis (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
5. Top 10 best-selling products by sales
6. Revenue by product category
7. Regional sales breakdown (additional insight visualization)
8. Written observations after each chart
9. Conclusion with 3 actionable business recommendations

## Key Findings
- Sales show strong Q4 seasonality with year-over-year growth
- Consumer segment drives ~$1.15M in sales, far ahead of Corporate and Home Office
- West and East regions outperform Central and South by a wide margin

## Tools Used
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Google Colab
